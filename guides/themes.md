# Themes

Muny follows your device's light or dark setting until you choose otherwise. Each person in a household picks their own theme, and it follows them to every device they sign in on.

## Choosing a theme

Go to **Settings → Appearance**.

- **Try it** shows the whole app in that theme without saving anything. Move around any page, then choose **Use this theme** or **Stop trying**.
- **Use** switches to that theme.
- **Match this device** goes back to following your device's light or dark setting.

## Making your own

Click **New theme**, or **Customize a copy** under any theme. Change the colors for the page, text, accent, meanings (good, bad, warning) and charts. The preview beside them updates as you go, using the same buttons, cards and charts as the rest of the app. **Try across the app** shows your unsaved theme everywhere, and keeps following your edits.

Muny warns you about combinations that are hard to read, like text with too little contrast against its background. It's your theme, so warnings don't stop you saving.

Custom themes are shared with everyone in your household, though each person still chooses which theme they use. If someone deletes the theme you're using, you go back to following your device.

Cards and charts take their colors from the theme, including the ones you build yourself, so you never need to think about colors when making a card.

## Sharing a theme, or asking an assistant for one

Under **Share, paste, or ask an AI assistant**, you can copy a theme as JSON or paste one in. A theme contains only a name and colors, so it's safe to paste from anywhere: Muny accepts nothing else. Ask an assistant for "a Muny theme as JSON" and show it the fields from the box on that page.
