Why Muny

The everyday tools of a money app, without the monthly bill

Commercial money apps work well, but they charge a subscription and keep everything you own on someone else's servers.

Yours, end to end

Runs on your NAS, home server or PC with a single SQLite file. No telemetry, nothing phoning home, and you can export everything.

Complete

Budgets, recurring bills, reports, net worth, loans, cards, savings, stocks and crypto, with alerts to your phone.

Built for AI, on your terms

An MCP server and REST API let assistants such as Claude read exactly what you allow, and suggest changes that wait for you.

Helps you spend less

Finds doubled-up subscriptions, price rises, card interest and idle cash, and tracks what you save.

Features

Everything in one place

Everyday money

  • Accounts from bank sync (Plaid or Teller, with your own keys), PayPal, or files
  • Import anything: Rocket Money exports, bank CSV/OFX/QFX/QBO, PDF statements, brokerage and Koinly exports
  • Transactions with search, filters, splits, notes, tags, merchant cleanup and bulk edits
  • Rules that file, rename, tag or hide transactions as they arrive
  • Recurring bills and subscriptions found automatically, with price rises
  • Budgets by category with rollover, alerts and a month-end forecast

Debts and wealth

  • Loans with payoff dates, payoff plans and statement reading
  • Credit cards with limits, statements, due dates and interest
  • Savings accounts with interest, earned rate and projections
  • Stocks from Plaid or broker exports, with cost basis and history
  • Crypto wallets and exchanges, with cost basis, gains and tax reports
  • Assets such as a home or car, for equity and net worth

Insights

  • A customizable Overview, arranged per screen size, with a card editor that calculates over all your data
  • Spending and reports: by category and merchant, month over month, net worth over time
  • Ways to save: doubled-up subscriptions, price rises, self-hosted replacements, card interest and idle cash
  • Taxes: yearly totals and an export for your accountant
  • Documents: statements and receipts, stored encrypted

AI and automation

  • MCP server and REST API with per-token permissions and an activity log
  • Approval inbox: assistants suggest changes; you approve or reject
  • Optional built-in AI (off by default) with Claude, an OpenAI-compatible server or Ollama
  • Alerts in the app, by phone push, ntfy, Apprise, email or webhook
  • Themes: light, dark and your own, with a live preview

AI assistants

Ask about your money. Approve what changes.

Connect an assistant like Claude over MCP. It reads only what its token allows, builds Overview cards and themes for you, and suggests changes that wait in your inbox. Muny never moves money.

  • Where did my money go this month compared with last?
  • Which subscriptions could I cut, and what would that save in a year?
  • Make me an Overview card showing dining spending by month against my budget.
  • Find my uncategorized transactions and suggest categories.
  • Nothing connects until you create a token, and each token only reaches the permissions you tick.
  • Suggestions wait for you. Each shows exactly what would change; approve or reject it.
  • Everything is logged, and a token can be revoked in one click.
Connect Claude Code (Muny shows you this command)
$ claude mcp add --transport http muny https://your-muny-address/mcp \
    --header "Authorization: Bearer muny_..."

Install

One container, one command

  1. You need Docker on any amd64 or arm64 machine.
  2. Run the commands, then open http://localhost:8420 and create your account.
  3. Keep a copy of .env somewhere safe, such as a password manager. Its key encrypts bank connections and stored keys.

Want to look around first? Add -e MUNY_DEMO=true and Muny starts with a made-up household. Settings → Data → Start clean removes it when you're ready.

Install guide (Compose, Synology, Unraid) 
# a folder for Muny's data
mkdir -p muny/data && cd muny

# the key that encrypts its secrets
umask 077; printf "MUNY_SECRET_KEY=%s\n" "$(openssl rand -base64 32)" > .env

docker run -d --name muny --restart unless-stopped --user "$(id -u):$(id -g)" \
  -p 8420:8420 --env-file .env -v "$PWD/data:/data" ghcr.io/mikedmor/muny:1
